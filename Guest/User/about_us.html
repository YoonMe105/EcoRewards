EcoRewards
EcoRewards is a dedicated sustainability rewards platform developed exclusively for Asia Pacific University (APU) students and staff. The platform is designed to encourage environmentally responsible behaviour across campus by transforming everyday eco-friendly actions into meaningful rewards.

Through EcoRewards, users can submit verified eco-friendly activities such as recycling, participation in sustainability initiatives, and involvement in campus environmental programs. Each approved submission earns eco points, which can be accumulated over time and redeemed for rewards available within the APU campus ecosystem.

By combining digital tracking, reward incentives, and community engagement, EcoRewards aims to foster a long-term culture of sustainability within APU while making environmental responsibility both rewarding and accessible.

Mission Statement:

Our mission is to cultivate a sustainable campus culture at APU by motivating students and staff to actively participate in eco-friendly practices. EcoRewards seeks to bridge awareness and action by rewarding positive environmental behaviour, reinforcing the idea that small actions can create meaningful impact when practiced collectively.

EcoRewards Helps Users To:

  • Track and submit eco-friendly actions easily
  • Earn eco points through verified sustainability efforts
  • Participate in campus eco events and initiatives
  • Redeem rewards available within the APU community
  • Develop long-term environmentally responsible habits

How It Works?

  1. Users perform eco-friendly actions on campus
  2. Actions are submitted through the EcoRewards platform
  3. Submissions are reviewed and verified
  4. Approved actions earn eco points
  5. Points can be redeemed for campus rewards
